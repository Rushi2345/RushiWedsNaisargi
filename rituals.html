The Chosen Day

Why Vasant Panchami?

On the fifth day of the bright half of Maha, spring arrives, mustard fields turn gold, and Maa Saraswati is worshipped. Families across Gujarat consider it one of the most auspicious days of the year to begin a new life together.

सरस्वति नमस्तुभ्यं वरदे कामरूपिणि ।
विद्यारम्भं करिष्यामि सिद्धिर्भवतु मे सदा ॥

Saraswati namastubhyam, varade kamarupini,
Vidyarambham karishyami, siddhir bhavatu me sada.

  • An abujh muhurat

    The day is auspicious in itself, so no separate muhurat needs to be searched for.

  • The day of Maa Saraswati

    Goddess of wisdom, music and speech, invoked so the couple's life is filled with understanding.

  • The first day of spring

    Yellow is the colour of the season: of mustard flowers, of haldi, and of new beginnings.

Rang ane Rivaj

Little traditions, big smiles

The moments in between the rituals are where the laughter lives. Keep an eye out for these.

At every ritual

Lagna Geet

The women of the family sing traditional wedding songs through every ceremony, each line carrying a blessing.

Between the families

Fatana

Playful teasing songs sung by one family about the other. Expect laughter, cheeky lines and quick replies from both sides.

During the pheras

Joota Chupai

The bride's sisters and cousins hide the groom's mojari, and return it only after a sweet bit of bargaining.

After the wedding

Aeki Beki

The couple search for a ring hidden in a bowl of milky water. Whoever finds it more often is said to rule the home.

At the new home

Gruh Pravesh

The bride enters her new home by gently tipping a kalash of rice with her foot, welcoming Lakshmi inside.

From the bride's mosal

Mameru

The bride's maternal uncle brings her the panetar saree, jewellery and gifts, wrapped in love from her mosal.
