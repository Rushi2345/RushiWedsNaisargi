Vivah Sanskar

The most sacred of the sixteen sanskars

In the Vedic tradition, a life is shaped by sixteen sanskars, and Vivah is the one that joins two lives into a single path. It is a promise made before Agni, the sacred fire, who stands as the eternal witness.

The bride and groom join hands in hast-melap, walk around the fire together, and take seven steps that turn two people into lifelong companions. From that moment they are partners in duty, in joy and in every season of life.

On Vasant Panchami, the day of Maa Saraswati and of spring's first bloom, Rushi and Naisargi begin this journey.
